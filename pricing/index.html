One flat fee. Quotable from memory. The figure is the figure - VAT is included and nothing is added on top.

Smaller blocks have a minimum of £3,600 a year. The accounts, the compliance calendar, the insurance and the consultations take much the same work whether a building has 6 flats or 40, so below about 10 flats the minimum is what applies.

What the annual fee covers

  • The full management service - the day-to-day running of the block, end to end.
  • Service-charge collection and supplier payments, with every invoice checked against contracted scope, prior invoices and a market comparator.
  • Statutory compliance - fire risk assessment, asbestos, electrical, water hygiene, lifts and fire doors, kept current on a tracked plan.
  • Section 20 consultation run properly, so the cost of major works stays recoverable.
  • A quarterly expenditure report to directors, and annual service-charge accounts filed on time and independently certified.
  • The leaseholder portal - every message, action and response logged and visible to the leaseholder it concerns.
  • Site visits at least monthly, arranged in advance so any director can walk the block with us, and as often as the block needs.
  • Active communication - you hear from us before you have to ask, and arrears are chased from the first missed payment.

For context, large managing agents commonly charge 10-15% to administer major works, and often use their own in-house surveyors. We use an independent external surveyor, appointed by the RTM. The firm running the works shouldn't also be the one pricing them.

Why it is priced this way

A fee that rises with spending rewards a manager for spending more of your money. A flat fee per unit does not, so our incentive is to run the block well rather than to run up the bill. We take no introduction fees or commissions from the contractors and partners we instruct, and no cut of the insurance premium. Every partner contracts directly with your company, so the agreement and the money sit between you and them.

Most London managing agents quote somewhere between £250 and £500 per unit per year, usually plus VAT, plus an uplift on major works and a list of per-document charges. Our number is inclusive of VAT with a short, published list of extras. For the service behind it - a career finance director, the portal, monthly site visits and every pound scrutinised - it is priced to be fair rather than to be the cheapest.
