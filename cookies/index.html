Cookies

Big Brain Ltd, trading as Proper Blocks · Last updated 1 August 2026

The portal uses two cookies, and only if they apply to you. Both are strictly necessary for signing in, so we don't ask your consent for them - ICO and PECR guidance permits strictly-necessary cookies without a banner.

The cookies we set

  • Name: session
  • Purpose: keeps you signed in between requests
  • Flags: HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax
  • Expires: 14 days after you sign in, or immediately when you sign out
  • Third parties: none - the cookie is only readable by our own server
  • Name: dh_trust
  • Purpose: set only if you tick "Remember me on this device for 30 days" when entering your two-factor code. It lets this browser skip the code screen. Your password is still typed and checked every time you sign in
  • Flags: HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax
  • Expires: 30 days after you tick the box, and using it does not extend that. It also stops working the moment you change or reset your password, turn two-factor off, or forget the device under Account > Remembered devices
  • Third parties: none. We store only a one-way scrambled copy of the cookie's value, plus a rough device label ("Windows PC - Chrome") so you can recognise it in that list

Cookies we do not set

  • No analytics (Google Analytics, Plausible, anything else).
  • No trackers, no pixels, no ad-tech.
  • No social-media share-button cookies.
  • No third-party CDNs that drop cookies (we use Cloudflare for hosting but not their analytics).

If we ever add an analytics cookie

We will add a consent banner before it runs, and you will be able to reject as easily as accept.
