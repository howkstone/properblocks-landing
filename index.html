How we work
Proper Blocks

We've lived the problem, and we know the two things that define a well-run block.

Financial diligence

Every pound the building spends is understood, accounted for, and worth what it cost. Service-charge debts pursued with strength and fairness, and the year-end service charge accounts always filed on time.

Reliable communication

Every message answered within two working days, and logged in the leaseholder portal where the person it concerns can see it. Director meetings minuted. Contractor visits and registrations handled through a documented flow. A regular newsletter for residents and directors.

And four things you can hold us to.

Written into how we work, for every block we manage.

Every invoice reviewed

No supplier invoice is auto-approved. We check each one against the work done and the lease before a pound leaves your account.

On site at least every month

A manager visits your building no less than once a month, arranged in advance so any director can walk it with us, and it can double as the board meeting. Small problems get caught in person, early.

A full report every quarter

Right to Manage directors get a written financial and operational update four times a year.

The accounts, open to you

Directors get a read-only login to the block's accounting records, so you can see the financial position at any time without asking for it. We reconcile the accounts monthly, so between reconciliations you'll see some recent items still to be posted.

I became the director in 2018, after over a decade of company turnarounds - small businesses in distress. Three things were key: sensible financial decisions, good communication, absolute persistence to put things right. I set up Proper Blocks to apply this to buildings, and help improve lives.

We're independent, so we only have a duty to the leaseholders of our blocks. We work with residential and mixed-use blocks across London, whatever your legal structure - RTM, Residents' Association, or organised leaseholders. The aim is always the same: a better-run block for everyone who owns, lives in or works there.

Taking us on, step by step.

Changing managing agent feels daunting. We run the handover ourselves, so the switch is orderly and you're never left exposed.

  1. 01

  2. 02
